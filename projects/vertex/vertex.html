Vertex

In high school, Along with two other students, I developed a multi-platform app that would connect the three most important educational stakeholders: parents, students, and teachers. The goal was to create a centralized platform that streamlined resource access and communication: Students can access information about their classes and clubs, staff can easily post updates and manage their classes, parents could report absences and be looped into school communication. We decided to call this app Vertex: the Point to Connect. We developed this multi-platform app following the Rapid Application Development model. We won third place for our project at the National level, and 1st place in Washington state, for the Mobile Application Development event in the FBLA (Future Business Leaders of America) competition.
